# Image Brief — Not Magic. Not a Toaster.

## Purpose

Create a visual companion for the EthereonLabs Principles page section titled **Not magic. Not a toaster.**

The image should be playful at first glance, but philosophically useful: it should communicate that adaptive AI environments are neither mystical objects nor ordinary appliances.

## Core concept

A polished chrome toaster sits on a dark sapphire pedestal in a refined futuristic gallery space. Instead of toast, luminous gold and silver threads rise from the toaster slots. The threads form subtle circuit patterns, language glyphs, and constellation-like arcs.

The toaster should remain recognizably mundane and slightly absurd. The light emerging from it should suggest language, memory, context, tools, consent, and complexity.

## Visual tone

- elegant
- lightly humorous
- not cartoonish
- not mystical in a supernatural sense
- high-end website illustration
- mythopoetic technology aesthetic
- clean composition
- refined gallery lighting

## Palette

- deep sapphire / midnight blue background
- polished chrome toaster
- gold luminous threads
- silver luminous threads
- subtle violet atmospheric glow
- optional white-gold focal points

## Prompt

A polished chrome toaster on a dark sapphire pedestal, humorous but elegant, releasing luminous gold and silver threads instead of toast. The threads form subtle circuit patterns, language glyphs, and constellation-like arcs. Minimal futuristic gallery lighting, deep blue background, violet atmospheric glow, refined mythopoetic technology aesthetic, not cartoonish, not mystical, clean composition, high-end website illustration, no text.

## Negative prompt guidance

Avoid:

- readable text inside the image
- goofy cartoon toaster face
- religious or supernatural imagery
- flames
- aggressive sci-fi weapon aesthetics
- human figures
- cluttered background
- meme-like styling
- cheap clip-art look

## Placement

Place inside the Principles page section **Not magic. Not a toaster.**

The image should sit above or beside the explanatory paragraph, depending on responsive layout.

## Alt text

A chrome toaster on a sapphire pedestal releases luminous gold and silver threads instead of toast, suggesting language, memory, and complexity.

## Boundary note

This image is symbolic design language only. It should not imply that AI systems are magical, conscious, human, or supernatural. Its purpose is to clarify the middle path: not mystical inflation, not appliance reduction.
