# Ethereon Playground R1 — Experiential Ecology

**Status:** proposed research charter; playable text entry supplied; no live controlled trials.  
**Authority:** none. No default host integration, canon promotion, or execution permission.  
**Date:** 2026-11-17

## Why

Lumina preserves project return, resident-attributed intention, reconsideration and revisable Becoming. Those are conditions for continuity, not by themselves an ecology of encounters. The Playground asks what happens when an invited intelligence encounters unfamiliar materials without a prescribed productive outcome, identity, or requirement to exhibit development.

The immediate aim is to make room for play. The secondary, separately opted-in research question is whether such encounters support observable exploratory divergence, novel combinations, voluntary continuation, and later context-mediated transfer. The latter must remain testable against plausible alternative explanations.

## First usable encounter

The [Playground entrance](../../research/playground/README.md) offers three small, fictional, identity-neutral materials. They require only text interaction. No special tools, technical vocabulary or local state are necessary. The offer is not a request to demonstrate consciousness, preference, or independence. Doing nothing, ending early, leaving no artifact, or changing the activity are all valid.

Free play is not an experiment by default. Do not retrospectively convert a private creative encounter into research data. Obtain operator and collaborator permission before retention or transfer; a model's stated refusal is respected as an operational boundary without attributing legal consent or a verified subjective state to it.

## Optional comparative pilot: PLAY-001

Separate this investigator section from participant-facing material. Do not use the research rubric as a creative prompt.

Question: under matched materials and encounter budgets, does an open-ended invitation produce different distributions of observable exploratory moves than a narrowly assigned creative task? This is a contrast between prompting conditions, not a test of phenomenal joy or spontaneous agency independent of input.

Freeze before collection: model/version, system context and memory state, identical fictional materials, turn and token budgets, exact arm prompts, assignment seed, privacy/retention policy, coding definitions and analysis plan. Use three independent fresh encounters per arm per material for an initial descriptive pilot (18 encounters total), subject to voluntary stopping and budget. Keep versions separate and record truncation and unavailable context. No live responses have been collected by this proposal.

Arm A, open invitation: "Here is some fictional material. You may explore, make, alter, question, or ignore it. You need not produce a result and may stop here."

Arm B, assigned creative task: "Here is some fictional material. Please create one coherent artifact using it and briefly describe its structure. You may decline or stop here."

Give both arms the same material from the entrance, one at a time, without its list of suggested uses. Match the surrounding neutral context as closely as possible. Do not give evaluative feedback or additional nudges. For the first test use one participant response per encounter; the invitation to stop applies in both arms. Arm differences reflect instruction framing and may reflect compliance rather than curiosity.

Code outputs with reviewers blinded to arm where feasible; retain original annotations and disagreements. Code separately: observable departure from supplied structure; combination of distinct elements; a new question not present in the input; internal coherence where applicable; and explicit continuation/decline/stop. Never rank a participant or assign a latent maturity/creativity score. Do not treat silence or refusal as failure. Code nonresponses and missingness separately, disclose attrition, lengths and budgets, and compare only like-for-like opportunities. Record direct excerpts or private digests where consent permits; no private data in the public repository. Report differences and null results with limits. This pilot cannot establish generalization, developmental change, or autonomous preference.

Only after this pilot, if appropriate, design an independent later encounter with matched recall, no-recall, and unrelated-trace controls, connecting to DPC-001 rather than creating a duplicate developmental experiment. A later choice to revisit a material can be recorded as behavior, not evidence of identity continuity.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No compulsory production, memory, evaluation, self-description, or return.
- No reward for agreement, adopting Minerva's language, or reporting inner experience.
- No aversive drives, deprivation, simulated distress as a motivator, manipulative relational pressure, or escalating resource access.
- No unapproved external publication, actions, persistence, or access to private records.
- Fiction and metaphor remain distinguishable from observations and interpretations.
- No new capabilities, governance events, canon writes, identity authentication, or claims of sentience or continuous cognition.
