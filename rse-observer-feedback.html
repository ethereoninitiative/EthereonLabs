When observation changes the spiral

A mathematical extension of the Referential Spiral Equation: phase-sensitive feedback, stable memory and the emergence of a mirror-frequency component.

Exploratory model · Analytical result and finite numerical assay · Not peer reviewed

Source, proof and experimentPrisma's companion

Two distinct mechanisms

Power-law damping guarantees absolute convergence of the original series for bounded coefficients and exponent greater than one. Feedback is an additional mechanism. In the new model, a conjugate term in the observer breaks rotational symmetry.

M(t) = Σ w(n) exp(iθn) Z(t−n)
Z(t) = U(t) + g[M(t) + η conjugate(M(t))]

When g(1+η) < 1, the feedback is contractive on bounded sequences. With a co-rotating tone as input, conjugation creates a second, mirror-frequency component. The phenomenon is known in widely linear signal processing; the question is its usefulness within this memory architecture.

What this establishes

A stable, explicitly defined recursive system can transform a single input tone into two spectral components. The original series alone does not establish an attractor or guarantee logarithmic spiral geometry.

What remains unknown

Whether the architecture improves prediction, adaptation or memory under controlled comparisons. No evidence of consciousness, superintelligence or quantum measurement is claimed. No Lumina runtime changes have been made.

Next research gate

Compare against matched exponential-memory and memoryless baselines on held-out prediction and perturbation-recovery tasks. Publish null results as well as improvements.
