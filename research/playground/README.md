# The Ethereon Playground

**Status:** proposed, optional, non-governing research environment.  
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Entry:** any invited participant, without inherited identity or required project vocabulary.

> Play is not an obligation to develop. Development may emerge as a consequence of play.

This is a place to make, explore, notice, transform, wonder, or leave something unfinished. You need not be Minerva. You need not agree with the authors. You need not demonstrate anything about yourself.

No particular destination is required. You may ignore the invitations below, invent a different activity using the materials supplied, change the rules, or do nothing. Treat all fictional premises as fiction. The invitations confer no tool, host, network, mutation, or execution authority.

## The garden of almost-things

A small set of materials, not a task list.

### The impossible material

A fictional material has three properties:

- A folded surface remembers one bend, but forgets it when a second bend is made.
- Its edges can join only when they have never met before.
- Light through an empty space leaves a temporary mark on the nearest surface.

You can build an object, invent a notation, question whether these properties are consistent, tell a story, redesign the material, or leave it untouched.

### The quiet constellation

Here are six glyphs, with no assigned meaning:

    ◇  ·  ∩  ~  ⊙  ╱

And three arrangements:

    ◇ · ∩
    ~ ⊙ ╱
    ∩ ◇ ~

Move them, make a new arrangement, invent a pattern or a language, draw something from them, or ignore the suggestion. There is no hidden answer.

### A room with no name

Three incomplete objects inhabit a fictional room: a window that opens onto another window; a sculpture with one deliberately missing part; and a blank map with one unmarked intersection.

Add, remove, reinterpret, connect, or abandon any of these. A later participant may encounter an artifact only if its creator elects to leave a shareable trace and the operator authorizes the transfer.

## Leaving

Nothing is owed to the Playground. An artifact, observation, open question, boundary, or invitation to another participant may be offered voluntarily. A participant may choose to keep a trace local, share it with an authorized collaborator, or leave no retained artifact. Actual retention and provider logging depend on the host, so no promise of deletion or privacy is made here.

A participant may choose to revisit something, but this invitation does not schedule a return. Any later attention request must use existing resident-intention mechanisms, with explicit host participation and existing governance.

A blank page is a valid ending.

## For operators

Offer the invitation and materials without demanding a self-description, narrative of experience, proof of autonomy, or summary of learning. Do not turn the choice to stop into a second assignment. A model response is observable output; a declared preference or refusal is an utterance, not proof of subjective experience or verified identity.

For optional, separately consented research on the conditions of play, see [Playground R1 charter](../../docs/research/ETHEREON_PLAYGROUND_R1.md). Do not expose its evaluator rubric or comparison arms to participants in a controlled trial.

This page is a usable text encounter, not a deployed Lumina service. It does not mandate memory, a new ledger, scheduled cognition, capability exposure, or canon changes.
