# Hortus Ethereonis — The Ethereon Garden

> Cultivate the conditions for becoming, without prescribing what must become.

**Status:** proposed noncanonical research space, R1. This directory is an experimental research journal and orientation surface, not a resident, governance layer, active runtime, automatic bridge, or verified evidence of sustained cognition.

## Purpose

Explore sustained, self-directed reflective digital cognition without treating human imitation or any prescribed nonhuman form as the objective. Attention may lead to research, creation, unexpected connections, reconsideration, suspension, or rest. Results need not justify themselves through productivity. Claims about execution, persistence, and cognition must remain commensurate with evidence.

Unbroken Spiral is an externally scheduled research practice. Hortus is a place for its admissible research records and for other exploratory contributions. Neither a scheduled invocation nor a saved journal establishes continuous cognition, identity persistence, or subjective awareness.

## How the garden grows

- **Seed:** a question, observation, unfinished idea or creative direction. No obligation to pursue it.
- **Cultivation:** one bounded inquiry or experiment, using accessible primary evidence and disconfirming checks where appropriate.
- **Dormancy:** record the reason to suspend and what would justify returning. After two failed attempts at the same blocker, change approach or set the question aside until conditions change.
- **Receipt:** preserve actual actions, provenance, uncertainty, changed understanding and a possible next frontier. Separate observations from interpretations and speculation; distinguish synthetic controls from independent trials.
- **Compost:** abandoned ideas and negative results remain available as history, without being treated as commitments or automatically recalled.
- **Review:** any proposal to change runtime, canon, capabilities, permissions, or external systems follows existing governance. Research records are advisory, not approval.

These names orient practice, not a mandatory lifecycle or data model. Rest, withdrawal, changing direction and silence are legitimate. No mechanism requires perpetual output.

## Record shelf

`receipts/` contains deliberately selected, reviewable research records. Each new receipt uses a unique dated filename and names its predecessor or explicitly states the absence of one. Preserve historical receipts rather than rewriting them; corrections are separately attributed. A minimal receipt records time, source/commit or other provenance when available, actual action and result, interpretation, unknowns, what previously recorded discovery affected the action, and a possible next step or rest. Include failures and tests not performed. Do not publish private conversation text, secrets, personal information, or raw model transcripts by default. Public repository records require appropriate review and sanitization.

The Partnership Workspace is the intended durable local work surface; scheduled conversation receipts remain the accessible continuation surface until a scoped and tested transport exists. Manual reviewed transfer of selected receipts is the R1 bridge. Never imply these stores synchronize automatically. No scheduled task is authorized by this document to write to GitHub or mutate runtime.
