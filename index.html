Why continuity

A conversation ends.
The work keeps unfolding.

A long creative or technical project carries more than files. It carries reasons, decisions, open questions, and the intention to return. Lumina is being built to give that history a durable, inspectable place.

01 / Context

Return with the thread.

The local runtime restores selected project state and records what happens next. Reviewed memory is checked against its source evidence before it can return as guidance.

02 / Intention

Keep the reason, too.

A durable journal preserves an intention’s origin, rationale, and later revisions. Intentions are recorded through explicit commands; they do not schedule or authorize actions.

03 / Difference

Let distinct voices remain.

Structured exchange experiments preserve attribution, uncertainty, and disagreement. A shared record can hold different positions without flattening them into a single voice.
