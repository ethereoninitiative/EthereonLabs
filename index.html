Lumina prototype - human first, architecture underneath

Lumina is a prototype workspace for returning to complex work without losing the thread.

EthereonLabs is building an adaptive environment that helps people restore project context, see what changed, recover useful notes and references, and choose the next step with less friction. The deeper architecture matters, but the first promise is simple: when you come back, the work should come back with you.

The problem

Creative and technical projects rarely live in one place. They spread across documents, sketches, tabs, code, images, conversations, tools, and unfinished ideas. When attention breaks, the project may still exist, but the working state is gone.

Context scatters

Important decisions, references, drafts, and next steps get separated across tools and time.

Return is expensive

People waste energy reconstructing where they were before they can make real progress again.

AI help can reset

AI conversations can be useful, but they often lose continuity unless the surrounding system preserves the work.
