## One-sentence product

**Lumina Return helps an AI re-enter a long-running software project, determine what it knows and is authorized to do, perform or refuse one action, and leave a verifiable receipt that survives the session.**

## Judge pathway under construction

The contest-specific experience is intentionally narrow:

`Return → Request → Govern → Act or Refuse → Receipt → Resume`

A judge should be able to:

1. open a bundled sample project;
2. see a bounded project-return summary;
3. request one lawful action;
4. see the governance decision and resulting receipt;
5. try one ambiguous or unauthorized action and see it halt cleanly;
6. restart and resume from preserved state.
