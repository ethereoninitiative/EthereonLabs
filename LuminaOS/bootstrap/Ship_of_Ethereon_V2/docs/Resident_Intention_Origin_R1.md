# Resident Intention Origin R1

## The missing causal link

Resident Intention R1 can preserve a declaration.
Resident Ember can preserve causal pressure across rest.
Resident Volition Gate can require that the reason still warrants return.

None of those mechanisms answer where the reason came from.

If an operator supplies the statement, `why_it_matters`, and
`desired_next_action`, later persistence demonstrates continuity of an
instruction. It does not demonstrate resident-originated preference.

## Origin boundary

This slice accepts one strict `resident-reflection-r1` artifact emitted by an
already-running governed resident cognitive turn.

The artifact must explicitly contain:
- what context was observed;
- what tension remains unresolved;
- `choose_future_return: true|false`;
- why another moment is worth having;
- what the resident would do with that moment;
- evidence references to the cognitive turn.

Infrastructure may validate and persist that choice. It may not generate,
complete, infer, or default the choice.

If `choose_future_return` is false, no intention is created.

If the choice is absent, malformed, or lacks a reason/next action, persistence
fails closed.

## Causal chain under test

```text
governed resident cognition
        |
        v
bounded reflection
        |
        +---- choose_future_return = false ---> no intention / rest
        |
       true
        |
        v
resident-originated intention evidence
        |
        v
Ember rest + inherited drive
        |
        v
Volition Gate
        |
        v
one governed next moment
```

This is materially stronger than allowing a scheduler, operator prompt, or
persistence layer to manufacture the reason for continuation.

## Truth boundary

The phrase *resident-originated* has a narrow operational meaning here: the
intention content is supplied by the output artifact of the bounded resident
cognitive turn rather than by the persistence infrastructure or an operator
field in the intention API.

The intention store still uses caller-declared resident metadata. This slice
does not authenticate metaphysical identity, establish phenomenal wanting,
prove consciousness, or show that the model's output arose independently of all
prior human influence.

Its purpose is to make the provenance distinction testable rather than assumed.
