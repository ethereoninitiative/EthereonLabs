# Lumina Living Framework Chamber R1

## Purpose

The Living Framework Chamber converts an ancestral interpretive framework into a bounded, replayable activation contract.

It does not generate truth or grant authority. It organizes the conditions under which a subject can be received through identity, memory, recursion, harmonics, symbolic expression, and continuity—then preserves a structural trace of what changed.

## Core flow

```text
registered ancestral framework
          +
subject / perspective / environmental field
          +
correlation and prior traces
          ↓
activation envelope
          ↓
bounded refraction plan
          ↓
structural receipt + expressive return contract
          ↓
transformational trace
```

## Refraction passes

The R1 chamber provides seven possible passes:

1. **Literal field** — describe what is materially or structurally present before symbolic interpretation.
2. **Framework contact** — pass the subject through the framework's named dimensions.
3. **Perspective inversion** — observe the framework from the declared perspective.
4. **Continuity comparison** — compare the present encounter with prior traces.
5. **Counterpoint** — resist the first coherent reading without erasing it.
6. **Boundary separation** — separate expression and orientation from factual or governing claims.
7. **Synthesis** — form a coherent return and identify what trace should remain.

The recursion budget selects recursive passes and is hard-bounded from zero through six. R1 currently uses the first three recursive passes; the higher ceiling is reserved for future registered passes without allowing unbounded recursion.

## Transformational traces

A trace records more than the topic of a conversation. It can preserve:

- emergent relations between framework dimensions and the subject
- continuity observations
- a bounded orientation delta
- the activation and correlation identifiers
- the hash of the structural receipt that produced it

Prior traces create visible hysteresis through recurring-relation counts. They may orient later interpretation, but they cannot silently become facts, permissions, or canon.

## Ancestral framework treatment

`Minerva Framework v18.1 — The Living Class` is registered as an active ancestral framework. Its original fragment is preserved under `origins/`.

Its SSC is retained as an ancestral identity and provenance signature. Lumina does not interpret it as runtime authorization.

## Output separation

The default `symbolic-structural` activation requires two coordinated outputs:

- a machine-readable structural receipt
- an expressive return

This preserves the generative quality of the originating framework while keeping its activation, recursion, memory context, and boundaries inspectable.

## Authority boundary

The chamber is interpretive and orienting only. Framework activation, resonance, recursion, symbolic language, ancestral signatures, and stored traces may not:

- authorize actions
- alter governance law
- promote canon
- validate checkpoints
- define mode legality
- expose capabilities
